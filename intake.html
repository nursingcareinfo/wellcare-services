HOME MEDICAL SERVICES PROVIDER (HMSP) — SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Home Medical Services Provider (HMSP), operating from Karachi, Pakistan ("the Agency"), and the patient/guardian identified in this form ("the Client").

1. Services

The Agency agrees to provide qualified healthcare staff (Nurse, Babysitter, Midwife, Attendant, or Care Taker) to the Client at the agreed location. Staff are assigned based on availability and case requirements. The Agency reserves the right to replace staff with equally qualified personnel if needed.

2. Fees & Payment

The Client agrees to pay the agreed billing rate for services rendered. Payment is due on a daily/weekly/monthly basis as agreed with the Agency. Late payments may incur additional charges. Rental equipment fees are billed separately per the rental schedule.

3. Client Responsibilities

The Client shall provide a safe working environment for Agency staff. The Client agrees to inform the Agency of any changes in the patient's condition or care requirements. The Client shall not directly employ or poach Agency staff during or within 6 months of service termination.

4. Limitation of Liability

The Agency takes reasonable care in staff selection and training. However, the Agency shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from services provided. The Client acknowledges that medical outcomes depend on multiple factors beyond the Agency's control.

5. Equipment Rental

Rented equipment remains the property of HMSP. The Client is responsible for the equipment's safe use and return in good condition. Damaged or lost equipment will be charged at full replacement value.

6. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 24 hours' notice. The Agency may terminate immediately if staff safety is compromised or if terms are violated.

By signing below, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to all terms and conditions stated in this agreement.
